# Audit Area

* [Audit area: architecture](architecture.md) - The one integration point Qrypt offers, what its documentation leaves undefined, the edge in front of it, the SDK that is not for entropy, and what the repository is missing before the first slice.
* [Audit area: compliance](compliance.md) - What has to stay private, what reaches a model provider, how long agent transcripts are kept, and what the project can log of its own Qrypt usage when Qrypt publishes no meter.
* [Audit area: data quality](data-quality.md) - The quality of the data a first-time developer or agent reads, Qrypt's documented contract and its examples, profiled by a committed script, with what is wrong, what is missing, what cannot be found and what agrees with the brief.
* [Audit area: performance](performance.md) - What bounds the quickstart's 3-second runtime and the agent's 10-minute target, from Qrypt's one documented region, its rate limit, the size of its answer and the cost of starting each runtime.
* [Audit area: security](security.md) - Where the Qrypt token and the generated key could leak in the first-run path, in Qrypt's own examples and in this project's agent test, and what the repository's scans can and cannot catch.
