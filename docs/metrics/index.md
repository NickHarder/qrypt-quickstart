# Metric

* [Activation: signup to first successful call](NS-01.md) - The share of a weekly cohort of new Qrypt accounts that get their first successful entropy response within 7 days of signup, stage 3 of the funnel.
* [Agent median time to first key](GR-02.md) - The median wall-clock minutes, over the successful runs of a round, from the brief's prompt to the agent's program printing the success line.
* [Agent success rate](DR-04.md) - The share of agent-readiness runs in which an AI coding agent, given only the quickstart and the brief's prompt, writes a program that prints the success line with a working key.
* [First key through the quickstart](DR-03.md) - The share of a weekly cohort of new Qrypt accounts whose first successful response to a quickstart snippet comes within 7 days of signup, stage 4 of the funnel.
* [Landing to signup](DR-01.md) - Of the quickstart page's weekly landings, the share that become a Qrypt account signed up through the quickstart's link, stage 1 of the funnel.
* [Return within 7 days](GR-01.md) - Of a weekly cohort's activated accounts, the share that make another successful entropy call between 24 hours and 7 days after their first, stage 5 of the funnel.
* [Token created](DR-02.md) - The share of a weekly cohort of new Qrypt accounts that hold an Entropy token within 7 days of signup, stage 2 of the funnel.
